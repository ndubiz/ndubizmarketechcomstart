A budget desk speaker with more than Bluetooth on the back.

What it does and why it is useful

A corded indoor speaker for desk, TV and room listening. IKEA lists optical and AUX inputs, an included optical cable, Spotify Tap and stereo pairing.

Useful features

  • Optical input offers an alternative to Bluetooth for a compatible TV.
  • Two units can be paired for stereo.
  • Three EQ settings help adjust the presentation.

Limits to check

  • Corded indoor design is not for battery-powered travel.
  • Multi-speaker mode plays the same mono signal; it is different from two-speaker stereo.

How to start using it

  1. Place the speaker near power and select Bluetooth, AUX or a supported optical source.
  2. For a TV, check that its optical output and audio settings match IKEA's instructions.
  3. Use the pairing instructions if adding a second unit; compare stereo and multi-speaker modes.
