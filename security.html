Security

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Ndubiz Market Tech takes reasonable steps to keep this site safe for visitors. This page explains what we do and what to watch out for.

Encrypted Connection

This site uses HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption) so that data sent between your browser and our server is encrypted in transit.

What We Don't Collect

We do not process payments or store credit card details on this site — purchases happen on the retailer's site (e.g. Amazon, Govee, Wyze, Meross) once you click through an affiliate link. We never ask for passwords, card numbers, or government ID numbers through a contact form or email.

Spotting a Fake or Impersonating Site

  • Our official website is ndubizmarketcomstart.com. The former ndubizmarketechcomstart.com address redirects here.
  • We will never email you asking for a password, card number, or urgent payment.
  • If you see this content mirrored on another domain or receive a suspicious message claiming to be from us, please report it here.

Third-Party Links

Affiliate and retailer links take you to third-party sites we don't control. Their own security practices apply once you leave our site — always check for HTTPS and a legitimate domain before entering payment details anywhere.

Reporting a Security Concern

If you believe you've found a security issue with this site, contact us and we'll look into it.
