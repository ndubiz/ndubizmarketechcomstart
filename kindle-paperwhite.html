What it does and why it may be useful

A light e-reader that is easy to read in bright sun and runs for weeks between charges.

Useful features

  • Light enough to carry in a coat pocket.

  • Battery lasts weeks, so there is nothing to charge in a hurry.

How to start using it

  1. Check how you will load books you already own that are not from Amazon.
