How to choose

Start with the job: unattended home monitoring needs a security camera. Meetings and streaming need a computer webcam. Then check power, mounting, privacy controls, storage costs and regional support. More resolution alone does not make a product the right fit.

Frequently asked questions

Is a webcam a replacement for a home security camera?

A webcam such as the Logitech C920 is intended for calls and recording through a computer. For home monitoring, compare cameras designed for alerts, storage and unattended use.

What should I check before choosing an outdoor camera?

Confirm the exact camera and power supply are rated for outdoor use. Check Wi-Fi coverage, mounting options, storage and whether the location provides suitable power.

Do I need a subscription for a smart camera?

It depends on the model and features you need. Compare included recording and alerts with paid cloud storage or advanced detection before buying.

How should I compare camera prices?

Include any storage card, power adapter, subscription or hub needed for your intended setup. Compare the complete setup rather than the camera price alone.
