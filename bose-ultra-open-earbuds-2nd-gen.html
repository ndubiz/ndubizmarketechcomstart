What it does and why it is useful

Open-ear cuff-style earbuds for everyday listening when you want to remain aware of conversations and surroundings. Their open design does not provide noise isolation.

Useful features

  • Bose lists up to nine hours of playback; actual runtime depends on settings.
  • Wireless charging and clearer-call features are included.
  • The cuff design leaves the ear canal open.

Limits to check

  • No active noise cancelling; background noise competes with your music.
  • IPX4 splash resistance is not waterproofing, and awareness is not a safety guarantee.

How to start using it

  1. Charge the case and fit each cuff using Bose's illustrated instructions.
  2. Pair with your phone and configure supported settings in the Bose app.
  3. Start at a moderate volume and test comfort before a long listening session.
