Choose the light, then the platform

For ordinary room lighting, start with a bulb that fits your fixture. For a visible colour feature, consider an accent light. For several coordinated rooms, compare the whole ecosystem and its hub requirements. A starter kit and a single light are not directly comparable on price; compare what you will actually install.
