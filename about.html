Who we are

A researcher's eye, turned toward everyday technology.

Ndubuisi Uko Ogbajie
Founder, Ndubiz Market Tech

Ndubuisi Uko Ogbajie has spent his career studying how human capital and economic wellness advance through technology — researching development and IT-driven progress on behalf of institutions working across borders. He holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Economics from the University of Port Harcourt, Nigeria, and has built business and merchandise partnerships with a West African conglomerate whose reach extends into Central Europe and beyond.

That background — equal parts economics, research, and international business — is what led to Ndubiz Market Tech. After years of studying how people and economies grow alongside technology, the natural next question became personal: which everyday tools actually deliver on that promise, in an ordinary home or a home office? This site is the answer to that question, built one honest review at a time.

Why AI, specifically

Artificial intelligence is the clearest example yet of technology's power to accelerate human capital — the same force Ndubuisi has spent years researching at an institutional level, now showing up in ordinary homes and workspaces. A well-chosen AI tool or smart device doesn't just save time; it's a small, personal instance of the economic advancement that used to only get discussed in research papers and policy rooms. That's the lens Ndubiz Market Tech reviews everything through: not "is this trendy," but "does this genuinely move someone forward."

We're not a massive editorial team — it's a small, hands-on operation that tests products directly, reads the fine print, and only recommends what we'd use ourselves.

What we cover

  • AI tools and apps for productivity and everyday tasks
  • Smart home devices — lighting, security, and automation
  • Home office & productivity gear — webcams, monitor lights, ergonomic accessories, and desk setups
