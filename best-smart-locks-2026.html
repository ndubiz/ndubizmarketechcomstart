Frequently asked questions

Will a smart lock fit my existing door?

Fit depends on the lock type, cylinder and door measurements. Check the exact manufacturer compatibility guide before choosing a retrofit lock or a replacement deadbolt.

Does a smart lock need Wi-Fi?

Connectivity varies by model. Local entry and remote control are different functions, so check which features work locally and whether remote access requires a hub.

What is the difference between retrofit and replacement locks?

A retrofit mechanism works with compatible existing lock hardware. A replacement model changes the deadbolt or lock assembly, so installation and door-fit requirements differ.

What should I check for backup access?

Check the exact model’s emergency power and backup-entry options before installation. Keep a practical entry plan for a depleted battery or an unavailable phone.
