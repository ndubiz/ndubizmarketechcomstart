What it does and why it may be useful

A small indoor plug-in camera with live view on your phone, two-way talk and Alexa support, aimed at an easy first security purchase.

Useful features

  • 1080p video with night vision for checking a doorway, a pet or a parcel.
  • Two-way talk through the speaker, so you can speak to whoever is there.
  • Works with Alexa, so it can double as a pet check and a doorway guard.

Limits to check

  • Needs USB power at all times; it cannot run on batteries.
  • Saving clips needs a Blink subscription, so budget for it if you want recordings.

How to start using it

  1. Pick a spot within reach of a USB power source and your Wi-Fi.
  2. Plug it in and set it up in the Blink app.
  3. Check the live view and the night view from where you will actually use it.
  4. Decide on the clip-storage subscription before you rely on saved recordings.

Who it may suit

Renters and first-time smart home buyers who want to check on a pet, a package or a front door without wiring anything. Skip or compare alternatives if you need a battery-powered camera, or you want saved clips without a subscription.

Questions before buying

Can it run on a battery?

No. It needs USB power, so place it near a socket or power source.

Do I need a subscription?

Saving clips needs a Blink subscription. Check Blink’s current plans and what the camera still does without one before buying.
