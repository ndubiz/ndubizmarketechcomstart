Wyze Lock Bolt Review

A fingerprint and keypad deadbolt for keyless entry.

The Wyze Lock Bolt replaces a compatible standard deadbolt and adds fingerprint, keypad, and Bluetooth app entry. It does not provide a traditional exterior keyhole, so confirm the fit and access options before ordering.

Pros & Cons

Good

  • Fast fingerprint and backlit-keypad entry
  • Cheaper than most competitors
  • Scheduled and one-time access codes in the Wyze app

Consider

  • Compatibility depends on your door — check first
  • Remote control requires a compatible Wyze hub; Bluetooth control is local

Our Verdict

Good smart-lock basics at a lower price than most competitors — just confirm your door is compatible before ordering.
