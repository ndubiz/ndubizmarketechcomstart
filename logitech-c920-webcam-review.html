The Logitech C920 is one of the most widely used external webcams for home offices, delivering sharp 1080p video and solid built-in stereo audio without needing extra software to get set up.

Pros & Cons

Good

  • Plug-and-play — works with most video call apps instantly
  • Sharp 1080p video, good in decent lighting
  • Well-reviewed and widely available

Consider

  • Low-light performance is average compared to newer models
  • No physical privacy shutter on some versions

Our Verdict

A dependable, no-fuss upgrade over a built-in laptop camera — a sensible first buy for a home office setup.
