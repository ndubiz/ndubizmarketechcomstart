What it does and why it is useful

A clip-on microphone for solo videos and interviews, with internal recording as a backup. Choose the kit for your phone or camera rather than buying a transmitter on its own.

Useful features

  • Internal recording gives you a separate audio file if the wireless connection drops.
  • Small wearable transmitters suit mobile filming.
  • Different kits support different phone and camera workflows.

Limits to check

  • 32-bit float cannot fix clothing noise, bad placement or an overloaded microphone capsule.
  • Recording settings, adapters and kit contents need checking before filming.

How to start using it

  1. Charge the transmitter and receiver and check the exact kit contents.
  2. Connect the receiver using the adapter or camera cable specified for your device.
  3. Clip the microphone away from rubbing fabric; enable internal recording and the intended format in the supported settings.
  4. Record and listen to a short test, then check that a backup file has been saved.
